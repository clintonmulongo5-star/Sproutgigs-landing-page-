What a task looks like

Here's an example of a typical microjob you might see on the task wall:

Watch a YouTube video and confirm you watched it

The task gives you the video link and thumbnail. You watch it in full, then submit proof — usually a screenshot of the video's end screen or a short comment showing you watched it, following whatever the task instructions specify.

Payout, time to complete, and exact proof required vary by task — always read the instructions on the task page before starting.
